LLM Fundamentals Transformer

No magic, no hidden reasoning: a series of mathematical operations repeated one word at a time. This page runs a real pass before your eyes, step by step.

Step 1 / 4

The sentence becomes tokens

The model does not read words. It splits the text into tokens: fragments (whole words, pieces of words, punctuation) drawn from a fixed vocabulary of about 100,000 entries. Each token gets a numeric identifier.

"The cat sleeps on the"
What the model actually receives: [976, 1723, 5089, 402, 496], a list of integers, nothing else.
Step 2 / 4

Each token becomes a vector

Each identifier is converted into a vector: a list of several hundred numbers that places the word in a geometric space. Words with similar meanings end up with similar vectors. This is where, and only where, the model "understands" something in the computational sense.

"cat" and "dog": close vectors. "cat" and "car": distant vectors. Geometric closeness is the notion of meaning, for the model.
Step 3 / 4

Attention weighs the context

Before producing the next word, the model has each token reread all the other tokens in the sentence, and calculates how much each one matters to it. This is the attention mechanism, the real heart of the Transformer.

■ = the tokens that "sleeps" looks at most strongly to find its place in the sentence
It is not a linear left-to-right reading. Each token looks at all the others simultaneously, with a different weight for each.
Step 4 / 4

A single word is chosen, by weighted chance

After the attention layers, the model produces a probability for every possible word in the vocabulary. There is no calculated "right answer": just a distribution. The next word is drawn according to these probabilities.

"The cat sleeps on the ? "
The chosen token is added to the sentence, and everything starts again at step 1 to predict the next token. An LLM never generates a whole sentence at once: it builds it one token at a time.
The full loop

A "reasoning" is just a repeated loop

What we perceive as a thoughtful answer is actually this same loop run hundreds of times, each new token lengthening the context that the model rereads in full.

tokenize→ vectorize→ attention→ probabilities→ draw 1 token↺

Why it matters to know this

What it is not

An LLM does not consult a database of facts, does not check anything while it writes, and has no fixed plan for the sentence before starting it.

What it is

A probability calculation on the next word, re-run at every token, where the only "memory" is the text already generated, reread in full each time.

Where errors come from

A hallucination is not an isolated bug: it is a sequence of plausible, statistically coherent tokens that corresponds to no verified fact.

Where coherence comes from

Each token takes all the previous ones into account through attention. That is what gives the illusion of a common thread, without any explicit plan.

One forward pass, four steps, repeated until the last token.
